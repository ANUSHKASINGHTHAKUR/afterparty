AFTERPARTY 2026-27 - SIMPLE SETUP GUIDE
=======================================
(The longer version is README.md. You do not need to edit any code.)

WHAT YOU GET
- A website where people pay by UPI, upload a screenshot + UTR, and register.
- An organiser page (use it on your phone) where you approve/reject and view tickets.
- After you approve, the person gets a personalised ticket with their name and a unique QR.
- A gate scanner page: first scan = VALID, second scan = ALREADY USED.

FILES YOU MAY WANT TO CHANGE (all optional)
1. .env                       -> your admin login and settings (START_HERE.bat creates it for you)
2. static/phonepe-qr.jpg      -> the payment QR (already your official QR)
3. static/ticket-original.jpg -> the ticket artwork (already your original)
Nothing else needs touching.

--------------------------------------------------------------
A) TRY IT ON YOUR LAPTOP (Windows)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install Python 3.10 or newer from python.org. TICK "Add Python to PATH".
2. Unzip the project folder (right-click > Extract All).
3. Double-click START_HERE.bat. First time it installs things (needs internet),
   then asks you to choose an admin username and password.
4. Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:5000  (works ONLY on your laptop - it is not public).
5. Organiser page: http://127.0.0.1:5000/admin

Want to play with fake data first? Double-click START_TEST_MODE.bat
(login: demo / demo-password-123, then press "Add 3 sample registrations").

--------------------------------------------------------------
B) PUT IT ONLINE (so phones can use it)
--------------------------------------------------------------
See README.md, section "Publish online". Short version:
- You need a host with HTTPS and a disk that is NOT erased (PythonAnywhere works; steps in README).
- Cost: a free plan exists but is limited; a paid plan (roughly $5-10 per month, check their pricing page)
  is safer while tickets are on sale.
- Set COOKIE_SECURE=1 in .env once the site is on https://.
- Camera scanning on phones ONLY works on the https:// website.
NOTE: The website is NOT online yet. A public link exists only after you (or I, with your hosting account) do this.

--------------------------------------------------------------
C) WHEN SELLING TICKETS (on your phone)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open https://YOUR-WEBSITE/admin and sign in.
2. Open your PhonePe / bank app and check the money really arrived for that UTR.
3. Tap Approve (or Reject). Approve = ticket and unique QR are created automatically.
4. The buyer sees the ticket on their private link. If they lose the link, they can use
   "Find my ticket" on the home page (reference + mobile number), or you tap "Download" and send it on WhatsApp.
5. Tap Backup on the dashboard every day and save the ZIP somewhere safe.

--------------------------------------------------------------
D) AT THE GATE (on a phone)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open https://YOUR-WEBSITE/admin/scanner, sign in, tap "Start camera", allow the camera.
2. Point at the ticket QR (phone screen or printout).
   GREEN  "VALID - ENTRY ALLOWED"            -> let them in (ticket is now marked USED)
   RED    "ALREADY USED - ENTRY DENIED"      -> do not allow
   RED    "INVALID TICKET" / "NOT APPROVED"  -> do not allow
3. Camera problem? Type the ticket number (APT-XXXXXXXX, printed on the ticket) in the box,
   tap Look up, then "Admit & mark as used".
4. Test the scanner on your own phone BEFORE event day with a test ticket.
